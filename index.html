Hi, I'm Foster.

I'm a graphic designer based in Ho, Ghana. I run VisualForge, my own design brand, and I'm the lead designer at tuta, an education startup. I also design for Genius IT Brainery as a volunteer.

I study ICT at Ho Technical University, so I care about how a design works on screen as well as how it looks. Most of my work is social media graphics and flyers, with brand design and mockups when a client needs a full look.
